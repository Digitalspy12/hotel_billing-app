# Hotel Ganesh – UI/UX Flow

## My Contribution

I contributed to the UI/UX planning and flow design of the Hotel Ganesh
Owner Billing App.

### Designed Flow

- Home screen with Dashboard, Tables and Menu
- Dashboard with Total Sales and Total Bills
- Table selection
- Menu category and dish selection
- Add items to Bill
- Bill summary and total amount
- Bill generation and preview
- Bill saving
- Bill History
- Today, Week, Month and Year filters
- Table-wise bill history

### Design Focus

The UI follows a simple red-and-white Hotel Ganesh theme and is designed
for an owner-only mobile billing workflow.
